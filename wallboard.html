Where the aircraft come from

Live aircraft from a free community ADS-B network, shown around your radar center (set in Location). No Raspberry Pi or antenna needed. For personal, non-commercial use.

Online service

If one service is slow or down, pick another. Data from adsb.lol (ODbL), airplanes.live or adsb.fi.

The address of your receiver's aircraft data. For tar1090 it usually ends in /tar1090/data/aircraft.json; for dump1090-fa, /dump1090-fa/data/aircraft.json. If the receiver is on another computer, use that computer's address, for example http://192.168.50.20/tar1090/data/aircraft.json.
